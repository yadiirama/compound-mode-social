1. The service

Compound Mode Social is a personal tool that provides a dashboard for the owner's YouTube channel (using Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) and TikTok account (using TikTok Login Kit OAuth 2.0 and the TikTok Display API). It is operated solely by and for its owner.

The App can display channel statistics and video inventory, and — when the owner authorizes publishing — upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

2. Acceptable use

The App is for personal use in managing the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It must not be used to access data from accounts the user does not own or manage, nor to circumvent the terms of service of YouTube, Google, or TikTok.

Video publishing is limited to the owner's own YouTube channel.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • YouTube: youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly, youtube.upload
  • TikTok: user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list (read-only)

The youtube.upload scope enables video publishing to the owner's channel. TikTok access remains strictly read-only: the App does not publish, upload, modify, or delete any content on TikTok.

Publication follows a defined autonomy policy approved by the owner: after an initial test publication with specific approval, the App may publish eligible content autonomously within the policy's limits. The owner retains full control and may revoke publishing authorization at any time.
