Compound Mode Social

Build Smarter. Grow Further.

Compound Mode Social is a personal social media management dashboard for the Compound Mode content brand. It connects read-only to the brand's YouTube channel and TikTok account to display channel statistics, published videos, and audience analytics in one place.

What it does

  • Channel overview — view subscriber and follower counts, total views, and video inventory.
  • Video listing — browse published videos with titles and publication dates.
  • Analytics summary — see views, watch time, and engagement trends.

How it connects to YouTube

The dashboard uses Google OAuth 2.0 to connect to your own YouTube channel. It requests read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly — view channel information and video listings.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — view YouTube Analytics reports.

How it connects to TikTok

The dashboard uses TikTok Login Kit OAuth 2.0 to connect to your own TikTok account. It requests read-only permissions:

  • user.info.basic — view account identifiers, display name, and avatar.
  • user.info.stats — view follower, following, like, and video counts.
  • video.list — view published videos and available metrics.

It never uploads videos, publishes content, posts comments, modifies content, or deletes anything on any platform. All data stays on your local machine; access tokens are stored in your operating system's secure credential store.
