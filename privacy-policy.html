Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Compound Mode Social ("the App") is a personal social media management tool that connects to the owner's YouTube channel through Google OAuth 2.0 and to the owner's TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit OAuth 2.0. This policy describes what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

2. Data accessed via Google APIs

The App requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — view YouTube channel metadata (channel name, ID, description), channel statistics (subscriber count, view count, video count), and the list of published videos (video IDs, titles, descriptions, publication dates, thumbnails).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly — view YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, subscriber changes) for the connected channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, set video metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy status), and schedule publication dates.

2a. YouTube upload and publishing

When the owner authorizes video publishing:

  • Video files are uploaded directly from the owner's Mac to YouTube via the official YouTube Data API v3. Video data is transmitted to Google's servers (*.googleapis.com) solely for publishing to the owner's channel.
  • The App sets video metadata as configured by the owner (title, description, privacy status, scheduled publication time).
  • The App does not post comments, modify channel settings, or delete content.
  • Publishing is performed only on the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Publication follows a defined autonomy policy: an initial test publication requires the owner's specific approval; once the owner approves the autonomy policy, the App may publish eligible content within the policy's limits (authorized accounts, content types, frequency caps, time windows). The owner may intervene at any time and revoke publishing authorization.

3. Data accessed via TikTok APIs

The App requests the following read-only TikTok Login Kit scopes:

  • user.info.basic — view basic TikTok account identifiers (open ID, union ID), display name, and profile avatar.
  • user.info.stats — view TikTok account statistics (follower count, following count, total likes received, video count).
  • video.list — view the list of videos published on the connected TikTok account (video IDs, titles, descriptions, creation dates, cover images, and available engagement metrics).

The App does not request user.info.profile and therefore cannot access bio descriptions, profile links, verification badges, or usernames. The App does not request any TikTok write permissions. It cannot upload videos, publish content, post comments, send messages, or delete content. The TikTok integration contains no video upload or publishing functionality.

4. How data is used

Accessed data is used solely to display the owner's YouTube channel statistics, video inventory, audience analytics, TikTok profile identifiers, account statistics, and published video listings in a local personal dashboard, and — when the owner authorizes publishing — to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. Data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

5. Data storage and security

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's Mac computer.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain (service MUSE). They are never written to plain-text files, never logged, and never transmitted to any server other than the official OAuth and API endpoints of Google (*.googleapis.com) and TikTok (*.tiktokapis.com, www.tiktok.com).
  • Video files prepared for upload are stored locally on the owner's Mac until publication. They are transmitted to Google only during the upload process.
  • TikTok Sandbox credentials are stored under dedicated Keychain identities (tiktok-sandbox-client-key, tiktok-sandbox-client-secret), separate from any production credentials.
  • API calls are executed on the owner's Mac. Tokens are never transmitted to external servers, analytics services, or the assistant's cloud infrastructure.
  • No data is sent to external analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

6. Data retention

Channel statistics, analytics snapshots, TikTok profile data, and video listings are retained locally for as long as the owner uses the App. Uploaded video metadata (publication records) is retained locally for operational tracking. The owner may delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the App's local data directory.

7. Revoking access

Publishing can be disabled at any time through the App's internal autonomy policy, without affecting read-only access.

The owner may revoke the App's Google access at any time via Google Account Permissions. After revocation, the App can no longer access YouTube data. Note: Google's account permissions page manages authorization at the app level; individual scopes cannot always be revoked separately.

The owner may revoke the App's TikTok access at any time via TikTok's account settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). After revocation, the App can no longer access TikTok data.

Locally stored tokens can be deleted from the macOS Keychain (service MUSE).

8. Data deletion

To request deletion of any data associated with the App, contact the owner at the address below. All locally stored data will be permanently deleted on request.

9. Children's privacy

The App is a personal tool not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the App's data practices. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.

11. Contact
